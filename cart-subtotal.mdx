Cart conditions look at the order as a whole rather than at individual products. They're the most common way to build free-shipping thresholds and weight-based rules.

<Note>
  This page covers cart conditions for **Shipping Rates**. For the customization equivalent, see [Cart Conditions](/cart-conditions) under Shipping Customizations — the available attributes differ.
</Note>

## Available attributes

| Attribute | Operators | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cart Quantity** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Greater Than, Less Than, Greater Than or Equal, Less Than or Equal | Number of items |
| **Cart Subtotal (total before discount)** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Greater Than, Less Than, Greater Than or Equal, Less Than or Equal | Amount in store currency |
| **Cart Total (total after discount)** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Greater Than, Less Than, Greater Than or Equal, Less Than or Equal | Amount in store currency |
| **Cart Weight** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Greater Than, Less Than, Greater Than or Equal, Less Than or Equal | Weight in your store's unit |
| **Cart Volume** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Greater Than, Less Than, Greater Than or Equal, Less Than or Equal | Volume in mm³ |
| **Cart Contains** | Depends on the sub-attribute you pick | See [Cart Contains](#cart-contains) |
| **Cart Does Not Contain** | Depends on the sub-attribute you pick | See [Cart Contains](#cart-contains) |
| **Cart Locale** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Contains, Does Not Contain | Shopify locale code |

## Subtotal before or after discount?

These two are easy to confuse, and picking the wrong one is a common source of surprise:

- **Cart Subtotal (total before discount)** — the cart value _ignoring_ discount codes.
- **Cart Total (total after discount)** — what the customer actually pays for goods.

<Warning>
  For a free-shipping threshold, decide deliberately which one you want. Using **Cart Total** means a discount code can drop a customer below your threshold and take their free shipping away at the last moment. Using **Cart Subtotal** means they keep it.
</Warning>

## Cart Contains

**Cart Contains** and **Cart Does Not Contain** are different from the other attributes: instead of comparing one cart-level number, they ask whether _any product in the cart_ matches a condition.

When you pick one, you then choose a product attribute to test against — Product Tag, Product Type, Product Metafield, and so on — and give it an operator and value, exactly as you would in a [product condition](/cart).

### Quantity and amount checks

**Cart Contains** adds one more control, letting you require a certain quantity or value of matching products rather than just their presence:

| Option | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Cart contains at least one product** | Default. Any matching product satisfies the condition. |
| **Cart contains more than X products** | Matching products must total more than X units. |
| **Cart contains less than X products** | Matching products must total fewer than X units. |
| **Cart contains more than X amount** | Matching products must total more than X in value. |
| **Cart contains less than X amount** | Matching products must total less than X in value. |

<Tip>
  **Cart Contains** is how you write "if the cart has any fragile item" without forcing the whole cart to be fragile. A plain [Product Tag](/cart) condition applies per line item; Cart Contains asks a question about the cart.
</Tip>

## Cart Locale

Matches the locale the customer is browsing in — useful for language-specific rates. Enter the Shopify locale code (`en`, `fr`, `de-DE`). You can enter several separated by semicolons.

## Examples

**Free shipping over \$100, before discounts are applied**

```text
Rule must match: All conditions
Condition 1: Cart Subtotal (total before discount) <> Greater Than or Equal <> 100
```

**A surcharge when the cart is heavy**

```text
Rule must match: All conditions
Condition 1: Cart Weight <> Greater Than <> 20
```

**A handling fee when the cart holds more than 3 fragile items**

```text
Rule must match: All conditions
Condition 1: Cart Contains <> Product Tag <> Equals <> fragile
             Quantity check: Cart contains more than X products, Quantity: 3
```
