Product conditions are evaluated **per line item**. A rule with a product condition matches when the items in the cart satisfy it — so these are what you use to separate bulky goods from regular ones, or to price by weight.

<Warning>
  Five of these attributes require [product syncing](/core-concepts/syncing-products) before they will work: **Product Tag**, **Product Type**, **Product Title**, **Product Barcode**, and **Product Compare-at Price**. Shopify does not send them to the app at checkout, so ShipMagic reads them from its own synced copy. Sync from **Settings → Products → Sync Now**.
</Warning>

## Available attributes

| Attribute | Operators | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product Title** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Contains, Does Not Contain | Multiple values separated by `;` |
| **Variant Title** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Contains, Does Not Contain | Multiple values separated by `;` |
| **Product Tag** | Equals, Does Not Equal | Multiple values separated by `,` |
| **SKU** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Contains, Does Not Contain | Multiple values separated by `;` |
| **Product Barcode** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Contains, Does Not Contain | Multiple values separated by `;` |
| **Product Vendor** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Contains, Does Not Contain | Multiple values separated by `;` |
| **Product Price** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Greater Than, Less Than, Greater Than or Equal, Less Than or Equal | Store currency |
| **Product Compare-at Price** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Greater Than, Less Than, Greater Than or Equal, Less Than or Equal | Store currency |
| **Product Quantity** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Greater Than, Less Than, Greater Than or Equal, Less Than or Equal | Units of that line item |
| **Product Type** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Contains, Does Not Contain | Multiple values separated by `;` |
| **Product Weight** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Greater Than, Less Than, Greater Than or Equal, Less Than or Equal | Store weight unit |
| **Product Metafield** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Contains, Greater Than, Less Than, Greater Than or Equal, Less Than or Equal, Is True, Is False | See [Metafields](#metafields) |
| **Variant Metafield** | The above, plus Is Empty and Is Not Empty | See [Metafields](#metafields) |
| **Line item property** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Contains, Does Not Contain | Multiple values separated by `;` |
| **Product Length / Width / Height** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Greater Than, Less Than, Greater Than or Equal, Less Than or Equal | Millimetres |
| **Product Volume** | Equals, Does Not Equal, Greater Than, Less Than, Greater Than or Equal, Less Than or Equal | mm³ |
| **Shopify Product ID** | Equals, Does Not Equal | Multiple values separated by `;` |
| **Shopify Variant ID** | Equals, Does Not Equal | Multiple values separated by `;` |

## Separators: commas or semicolons?

This trips people up, because it isn't consistent — it follows what the data itself can contain.

- **Product Tag** takes **commas**: `bulky,oversized,freight`
- **Everything else** takes **semicolons**: `Blue Sofa;Red Sofa`

<Tip>
  Titles and types can legitimately contain a comma ("Sofa, Large"), which is why they use semicolons. Tags cannot contain commas in Shopify, so commas are safe there.
</Tip>

## Metafields

For **Product Metafield**, enter the metafield key. For **Variant Metafield**, enter it as `Namespace.Key`.

Metafield conditions support type-aware operators — numeric comparisons for number fields, `Is True` / `Is False` for booleans, and `Is Empty` / `Is Not Empty` on variant metafields to test whether a value has been set at all.

<Note>
  Metafields can also be used to _set the price itself_, not just to match. See [Rates from Metafields](/rates-from-metafields).
</Note>

## Dimensions

**Length**, **Width**, **Height**, and **Volume** are always in **millimetres** (volume in mm³), regardless of your store's display units. They come from the dimension data on the product — see [Adding Dimensions to Products](/adding-dimensions-to-products) for how to populate them.

## Per line item vs. per cart

A product condition asks about the items in the cart. If you need to ask whether the cart _contains_ a matching product without requiring every product to match, use **Cart Contains** instead — see [Cart Conditions](/cart-subtotal#cart-contains).

## Examples

**Separate bulky products from the rest**

```text
Rule 1 — Bulky
Condition 1: Product Tag <> Equals <> bulky

Rule 2 — Regular
Condition 1: Product Tag <> Does Not Equal <> bulky
```

**A freight rate for anything longer than 2 metres**

```text
Condition 1: Product Length <> Greater Than <> 2000
```

**Match several vendors at once**

```text
Condition 1: Product Vendor <> Equals <> Acme;Globex;Initech
```
