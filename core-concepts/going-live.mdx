Once you’ve tested your setup and are confident everything is working correctly, you’re ready to go live and start showing ShipMagic rates to your customers.

---

## Steps to Go Live

### 1. Subscribe to a Paid Plan

To activate ShipMagic for live customers, you need to subscribe to one of the available plans.

[Choose a plan](/core-concepts/billing) based on your usage:

- **Starter Plan** → For Shipping Customizations only
- **Pro Plan** → For Shipping Rates (includes customizations)
- **Pro\+ Plan** → For advanced or custom-coded logic

---

### 2. Enable ShipMagic

1. Go to the **Settings** page in the app
2. Turn on the toggles for shipping rates / shipping customizations
   <Frame>
     ![Screenshot 2026 05 06 At 9 11 36 PM](/images/Screenshot-2026-05-06-at-9.11.36-PM.png)
   </Frame>

Once enabled, ShipMagic will start applying your rules at checkout for all customers.

---

## Before You Go Live (Checklist)

Make sure you’ve completed the following:

- ✅ Tested all key scenarios (products, cart values, locations)
- ✅ Synced product data (if required)
- ✅ Confirmed correct rates are showing in test mode

---

## What Happens After Going Live?

- Customers will see **ShipMagic-powered rates** at checkout
- Your configured rules will run in real time
- Test mode restrictions (like using “shipmagic” as first name) will no longer apply

---

## Removing Old Shopify Rates

If your existing Shopify shipping rates are showing alongside ShipMagic rates, you may want to remove them to avoid confusion at checkout.

### How to fix this

- Go to **Shopify Admin → Settings → Shipping and Delivery**
- Open your shipping profile
- Locate the existing (manual) shipping rates
- Delete the old rates

Once removed, only ShipMagic rates will be shown to your customers, ensuring a clean and accurate checkout experience.

---

## Need to Make Changes?

You can update your rules anytime. Changes are reflected instantly, so you can continuously optimize your shipping setup. Just make sure to use a new random address for testing to avoid seeing cached shipping rates.
