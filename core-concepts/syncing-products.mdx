## Why do you need to sync product data?

Some product attributes are not available in real time during shipping rate calculation at checkout.

If your shipping rules depend on these attributes, ShipMagic needs to store that product data in advance so it can be used when rates are calculated. That is why product syncing is required.

## When you need to sync product data?

You only need to sync product data when creating **Shipping Rate** rules based on any of the following product attributes:

- Product Tag
- Product Type
- Product Title
- Product Barcode
- Product Compare-at Price

<Note>
  Product syncing is **NOT required** for **Shipping Customizations**.
</Note>

## How to sync product data?

1. Open the **Settings** page in the app
2. Click **“Sync Now”** under **Products**
3. Syncing will run in the background, and you can safely leave the page

## Automatic syncing of product data

ShipMagic can keep your synced products up to date in two ways. They work well together.

### Scheduled Product Sync

Re-syncs your whole catalogue automatically once a **week**. Each run picks up changes to existing products (tags, title, type, barcode, compare-at price) and removes products you have deleted in Shopify.

1. Open the **Settings** page in the app
2. Under **Products → Scheduled Product Sync**, choose **Weekly**
3. Click **Save**

Scheduled syncs run overnight, between 1am and 5am in your store's timezone. The first one runs the night you turn it on, then every week on the same day and at the same time. Settings shows when the next one is due.

<Note>
  Scheduled Product Sync is available on the **Pro** and **Pro+** plans. See [Billing](/core-concepts/billing).
</Note>

### Auto Product Sync

Turn on **Auto Product Sync** from the Settings page to sync **newly created** products as soon as you add them.

**Important Notes:**

- Only **newly created products** are synced immediately
- Changes to existing products are picked up by the next **scheduled sync**. If you need a change to apply straight away, run **Sync Now** from the Settings page

## If a sync fails

If a sync doesn't finish, Settings shows **Last sync failed** under **Sync Now**, with the reason. Run **Sync Now** again. Your previously synced products stay in place until a sync completes.

Stores with more than **80,000 variants** can't use product syncing.

## Bottomline

If your shipping rate rules use **Product Tag, Type, Title, Barcode, or Compare-at Price**, make sure your products are synced before testing or going live.
