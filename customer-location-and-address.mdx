Location conditions are how you build regional pricing — remote-area surcharges, island rates, metro vs. rural, or restricting a rate to certain countries.

## Available attributes

| Attribute | Operators | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Zipcode / Postcode** | Equals, Does Not Equal | Wildcards and ranges supported — see below |
| **Province / State Code** | Equals, Does Not Equal | Two-letter Shopify province codes, separated by `,` |
| **Address Line 1** | Contains, Does Not Contain | Multiple values separated by `;` |
| **Country Code** | Equals, Does Not Equal | Two-letter ISO codes, separated by `;` |
| **Warehouse Zipcode / Postcode** | Equals, Does Not Equal | Same wildcard and range syntax |
| **Time of the Day** | Greater Than, Less Than, Greater Than or Equal, Less Than or Equal | Store timezone |
| **Day of the Week** | Equals, Does Not Equal | Full day names, separated by `,` |

## Postcode matching

The postcode fields accept three syntaxes, and you can mix them in one condition.

| Syntax | Example | Matches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact | `90210` | That postcode only |
| Wildcard | `AB14*` | Anything starting `AB14` |
| Range | `90209-90231` | Every postcode numerically within the range |
| Multiple | `AB14*,90210,3000-3999` | Any of the above, separated by commas |

<Tip>
  Wildcards are the practical choice for UK, Canadian, and Dutch postcodes, where the first characters identify the area. Ranges suit numeric systems like US ZIP and Australian postcodes.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Ranges compare numerically. A range like `AB1-AB9` will not behave as you expect on alphanumeric postcodes — use wildcards there instead.
</Warning>

## Country and province codes

Both take the two-letter codes Shopify uses, not full names.

- **Country Code** — ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 (`US`, `GB`, `AU`, `IN`). Separate multiple with semicolons. [Full list](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2023-10/enums/CountryCode).
- **Province / State Code** — two-letter province codes (`CA`, `NY`, `VIC`, `MH`). Separate multiple with commas. [Full list](https://matrixify.app/documentation/shopify-address-countries-and-provinces/).

## Warehouse Zipcode

Matches the **origin** rather than the destination — the location the order would ship from. Use it when you have several warehouses and want rates to differ depending on which one fulfils the order.

<Note>
  This pairs with [preventing double shipping charges](/avoid-doubling-of-shipping-charges-due-to-multiple-locations) when an order splits across locations.
</Note>

## Time and day

**Time of the Day** and **Day of the Week** are evaluated in your **store's timezone**, not the customer's.

Day names are entered in full and separated by commas:

```text
Condition 1: Day of the Week <> Equals <> Saturday,Sunday
```

<Tip>
  Use these for cut-off logic — for example, hiding an express option after 2pm, or over the weekend when your warehouse is closed.
</Tip>

## Examples

**A remote-area surcharge for specific postcode ranges**

```text
Rule must match: All conditions
Condition 1: Zipcode / Postcode <> Equals <> 4800-4899,6700-6799
```

**Restrict a rate to mainland UK by excluding island postcodes**

```text
Rule must match: All conditions
Condition 1: Country Code <> Equals <> GB
Condition 2: Zipcode / Postcode <> Does Not Equal <> BT*,IM*,JE*,GY*,HS*,ZE*
```

**No weekend express delivery**

```text
Rule must match: All conditions
Condition 1: Day of the Week <> Does Not Equal <> Saturday,Sunday
```
