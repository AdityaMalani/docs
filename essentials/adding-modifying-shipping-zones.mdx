Shipping zones live in **Shopify**, not in ShipMagic. The app reads a copy of them so you can attach rates to each zone inside a rule.

## Syncing zones

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your zones in Shopify">
    Go to **Shopify Admin → Settings → Shipping and Delivery** and create the zones you need on the relevant shipping profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync them into ShipMagic">
    Open the **Settings** page in the app, find **Shipping Zones**, and click **Sync now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it to finish">
    The button shows a loading state while syncing. When it completes, the page shows **Last synced** with a timestamp.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once synced, every zone appears as its own section under **Shipping zones** when you create or edit a rule, each with its own **Add rate** action.

## When you need to re-sync

Zones are not synced automatically. Run a manual sync whenever you:

- Add a new shipping zone in Shopify
- Rename an existing zone
- Delete a zone
- Add ShipMagic as a rate provider to a new shipping profile

<Warning>
  If you add a zone in Shopify and don't re-sync, it won't appear in the rule editor and you can't attach rates to it — customers in that zone will see no ShipMagic rates.
</Warning>

## If no zones appear

The rule editor shows a warning banner instead of your zones, with a link to the settings page. This means either the sync hasn't been run, or ShipMagic isn't attached as a rate provider yet.

Check both:

1. **Settings → Shipping Zones → Sync now** in the app.
2. **Shopify Admin → Settings → Shipping and Delivery** — confirm ShipMagic is listed as a rate provider on the profile. See the [Installation Guide](/installation-guide#add-shipmagic-as-a-rate-provider-custom-profiles--zones) for custom profiles.

## Zones with no rates

A synced zone that has no rates attached shows a warning inside the rule:

> No rates. Customers in this zone won't see shipping rates from ShipMagic app.

This is worth taking seriously. A rule can match a customer's cart perfectly and still return nothing if their zone has no rate attached.

<Tip>
  When you add a new country to an existing zone in Shopify, no re-sync is needed — the zone already exists and its rates carry over. Re-sync only when the zone list itself changes.
</Tip>

## Custom shipping profiles

ShipMagic connects to the **General** profile automatically. Custom profiles need the app added manually as a rate provider, and then a zone sync so the app picks up their zones.
