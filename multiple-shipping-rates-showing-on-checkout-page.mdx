Too many shipping options is a conversion problem, not just an aesthetic one. Here's where the extras usually come from.

## 1. Old Shopify rates alongside ShipMagic rates

The most common cause. Your manual Shopify rates don't disappear when ShipMagic starts returning its own — both are offered.

**Check:** **Shopify Admin → Settings → Shipping and Delivery**, and look at the manual rates in your profile.

**Fix:** delete the manual rates you no longer want. See [Going Live](/core-concepts/going-live#removing-old-shopify-rates).

## 2. Several rules matching the same cart

Every matching rule contributes its rates. Three rules matching one cart produce three sets.

**Fix, depending on what you want:**

- **They should combine into one price** — give the rates the **same name** and set basic blending to **Add rates**. See [Rate Blending](/rate-blending).
- **Only one should apply** — mark the winning rule [Exclusive](/exclusive-rules) so lower-priority rules are skipped.
- **They're genuinely alternatives** — set blending to **lowest** or **highest** so only one shows.

<Tip>
  Blending only merges rates with **matching names**. Two rates that should combine but are called "Standard Shipping" and "Standard Delivery" will stubbornly stay separate. Check the names first.
</Tip>

## 3. Ranged rate bands overlapping

If two [ranged rates](/ranged-rates) cover the same value, a cart in the overlap matches both.

**Check:** your band boundaries. Bands `0–100` and `100–200` overlap at exactly 100.

**Fix:** start each band just above the previous one — `0–100`, then `100.01–200`.

## 4. A carrier returning many services

Live carrier rates can return a long list — Shiprocket in particular queries many courier partners, and ShipStation exposes every carrier in your account.

**Fix:**

- Use **Lowest rate only** or **Highest rate only** on the carrier rate.
- Select fewer services in the carrier's configuration.
- Hide the extras with a [customization](/ai-tools/actions-hide-rename-sort).

## 5. Rates duplicated across zones

If a destination falls into two overlapping zones, both zones' rates can appear.

**Check:** your zone definitions in Shopify for overlapping country or region coverage.

## 6. A multi-location order charging twice

An order fulfilled from two locations can produce shipping for each.

**Fix:** see [Prevent double shipping charges](/avoid-doubling-of-shipping-charges-due-to-multiple-locations).

## Reducing the list deliberately

Once the duplicates are gone, decide how many options you actually want to offer.

<Tip>
  Two or three is the practical sweet spot — typically a standard option, a faster one, and free shipping if you offer it. Beyond that, extra choices tend to cause hesitation rather than satisfaction.
</Tip>

To trim without changing your rate logic, use **Only show specified shipping rates and hide rest** in a customization. It settles the outcome in one rule and keeps working as new methods appear.
