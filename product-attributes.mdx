Product conditions ask whether the items in the cart meet a test. They're how you withdraw a shipping method for goods that can't travel that way — fragile items, hazardous goods, oversized furniture.

<Note>
  Unlike shipping rates, customizations **do not require product syncing**. Shopify sends product data to customizations at checkout directly.
</Note>

## Available attributes

| Attribute | Parameter set | Operators |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product Tag** | 1, 2 | Equals, Not equals |
| **Product Title** | 1 | Equals, Not equals, Contains, Does not contains, Includes in list, Not includes in list |
| **Product Type** | 1 | Equals, Not equals, Contains, Does not contains, Includes in list, Not includes in list |
| **Product Vendor** | 1 | Equals, Not equals, Contains, Does not contains, Includes in list, Not includes in list |
| **Variant Title** | 1 | Equals, Not equals, Contains, Does not contains, Includes in list, Not includes in list |
| **Product Weight** | 1 | Numeric comparisons |
| **Product Price** | 1 | Numeric comparisons, Is empty, Is not empty |
| **Product Collection** | 1, 3 | Equals, Not equals |
| **Line Item Property** | 1 | Equals, Not equals, Contains, Does not contains, Is empty, Is not empty |
| **SKU** | 2 | Equals, Not equals, Contains, Does not contains, Includes in list, Not includes in list |
| **Variant Metafield** | 2 | Equals, Not equals, Contains, Does not contains |
| **Is subscription product** | 2 | True, False |
| **Product Metafield** | 3 | Equals, Not equals, Contains, Does not contains, Is empty, Is not empty, Includes in list, Not includes in list |
| **Number Of Product Metafield Values** | 3 | Numeric comparisons |

<Warning>
  Attributes from different sets cannot be combined in one rule. SKU (set 2) and Product Metafield (set 3) will never appear in the same dropdown. See [Parameter Sets](/ai-tools/different-parameter-sets-for-conditions).
</Warning>

## Product Collection

Collections are matched by **ID**, not by name. Enter comma-separated IDs.

ShipMagic keeps a short guide on locating them: [How to find the ID of a product collection](https://shipmagic.tawk.help/article/how-to-find-the-id-of-a-product-collection).

<Tip>
  Collections are often the most maintainable way to drive shipping rules. Merchandisers already curate them, and a product joining the collection automatically picks up the shipping behaviour — no tag to remember.
</Tip>

## How the cart is evaluated

For each product condition you also choose how much of the cart must satisfy it:

| Option | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **All products meet the condition** | Every line item must match. |
| **At least one product meets the condition** | Any single line item matching is enough. |
| **Cart has exactly specified quantity** | Matching items total exactly N units. |
| **Cart has more than specified quantity** | Matching items total more than N units. |
| **Cart has not more than specified quantity** | Matching items total no more than N units. |
| **Cart has more than specified total price** | Matching items exceed N in value. |
| **Cart has not more than total price** | Matching items do not exceed N in value. |

<Note>
  This choice is what makes product conditions precise. "Hide express if **any** item is fragile" and "hide express if **all** items are fragile" are very different rules — pick deliberately.
</Note>

## Includes in list

The `Includes in list` and `Not includes in list` operators test membership against a list of values, rather than a single comparison. Use them when you have a set of SKUs or titles to match and don't want a condition per value.

## Number Of Product Metafield Values

Counts how many **distinct values** of a given product metafield appear across the cart.

<Note>
  Products with no value for the metafield are not counted.
</Note>

This is the tool for mixed-cart problems — for example, hiding a consolidated shipping option when the cart draws from more than one warehouse or supplier.

## Examples

**No express delivery for fragile items**

```text
Type: Hide shipping rates
Condition 1: Product Tag <> Equals <> fragile
             At least one product meets the condition.
Hide the following shipping methods: Express
```

**Freight only for a furniture collection**

```text
Type: Only show specified shipping rates and hide rest
Condition 1: Product Collection <> Equals <> 123456789
             At least one product meets the condition.
Show the following shipping methods: Freight Delivery
```

**Subscriptions ship one way only**

```text
Type: Only show specified shipping rates and hide rest
Condition 1: Is subscription product <> True
Show the following shipping methods: Subscription Delivery
Apply rule to: Only the first shipment
```
