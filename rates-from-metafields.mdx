Instead of expressing shipping in rules, you can store a price directly on each product or variant as a metafield and have ShipMagic read it at checkout. This suits catalogues where every item has its own negotiated shipping cost that no formula would capture.

## The four options

Set **Calculation parameter** to one of:

| Parameter | Reads from | Behaviour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product Metafield** | A product-level metafield | Uses the value from the matching product |
| **Variant Metafield** | A variant-level metafield | Uses the value from the matching variant |
| **Sum Of Product Metafield** | Product-level metafields | Adds the values across every item in the cart |
| **Sum Of Variant Metafield** | Variant-level metafields | Adds the values across every item in the cart |

The **Sum Of** variants are what you want when each product carries its own shipping cost and the order total should be the sum of them.

## Setting one up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the metafield in Shopify">
    In **Settings → Custom data**, add a metafield definition on Product or Variant. Give it a numeric type and populate it on your products.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a rate and pick the parameter">
    In your rule, click **Add rate** and set **Calculation parameter** to one of the four options above.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the metafield reference">
    - For a **product** metafield, enter the **Key**.
    - For a **variant** metafield, enter it as **`Namespace.Key`**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide whether to multiply by quantity">
    Tick **Increase rate per item** if the stored value is a _per-unit_ price that should be multiplied by how many the customer bought. Leave it off if the value is a flat charge for that product regardless of quantity.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a default rate">
    Enter a **Default rate**. This is used whenever a product or variant has no value in the metafield.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Always set a default rate

The **Default rate** is the single most important field on this form.

<Warning>
  If a product has no value in the metafield and no default rate is set, that product contributes nothing to the shipping calculation. On a **Sum Of** rate this quietly undercharges; on a single-product rate the customer may see no shipping option at all.

  Set a default rate that is safe to charge for an unconfigured product.
</Warning>

Any new product added to your catalogue starts with an empty metafield, so the default rate is what protects you between a product going live and someone remembering to fill in its shipping cost.

## Keeping the data current

Metafield values are read at checkout, so edits in Shopify take effect immediately — there is no sync step for metafield _values_.

<Note>
  This is different from [product syncing](/core-concepts/syncing-products), which applies to tags, type, title, barcode, and compare-at price used in **conditions**. Metafield rate values are read live.
</Note>

## Examples

**Each variant carries its own shipping cost**

```text
Rate name: Standard Shipping
Calculation parameter: Sum Of Variant Metafield
Namespace and key: shipping.cost
Increase rate per item: on
Default rate: 9.95
```

Each line item contributes its metafield value multiplied by its quantity, and those totals are added together. A cart holding one variant at `4.00` and two of a variant at `6.00` produces `4.00 + (6.00 × 2)` = `16.00`.

**A flat per-product charge, not multiplied by quantity**

```text
Calculation parameter: Sum Of Product Metafield
Key: shipping_surcharge
Increase rate per item: off
Default rate: 0
```
