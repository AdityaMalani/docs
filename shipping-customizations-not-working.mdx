---
title: "Shipping Customizations Not Working"
description: "Work through the causes of a hide, show, rename, or reorder rule not taking effect"
icon: "circle-exclamation"
---

Customizations don't need CCS and don't need product syncing, which rules out two common classes of problem. What's left is usually conditions, method names, or mode.

## 1. Is the customization active?

**Check:** open the customization and confirm **Status** is _Active_.

## 2. Are you in test mode without the test name?

In test mode, customizations only apply when the checkout's **first name is `shipmagic`**.

**Check:** **Settings → App Modes** — the Shipping Customizations toggle.

**Fix:** use `shipmagic` as the first name when testing, or [go live](/core-concepts/going-live). Note the toggles are independent — rates can be live while customizations are still in test mode.

## 3. Does the method name match?

This is the most common cause of a rule that looks right but does nothing.

Method names match **partially**, but they must match _something_. `Epxress` matches nothing; `Express` matches "Express Shipping".

**Check:** compare the name in your rule against the name shown at checkout, character for character.

**Fix:** copy the name from checkout rather than typing it from memory.

## 4. Do the conditions match?

**Check each one:**

- Is **All conditions** set where you meant **Any condition**?
- For **customer** conditions — is the customer logged in? A guest has no tags, zero spend, and zero orders.
- For **Product Collection** — did you enter the collection **ID** rather than its name?
- For product conditions — is the cart-evaluation mode right? _All products meet the condition_ and _At least one product meets the condition_ behave very differently.

## 5. Are two customizations fighting?

All customization rules apply together. One rule hiding a method and another showing it can produce results that look random until you trace the order.

**Check:** list every customization that could match your test cart and reason through them together.

## 6. Is the method there to act on?

A hide rule can only hide something that exists. If the underlying rate isn't being generated in the first place, the customization has nothing to do.

**Check:** temporarily deactivate the customization and confirm the method appears at all. If it doesn't, the problem is on the rates side — see [Shipping Rates Not Showing](/shipping-rates-not-showing-on-checkout-page).
