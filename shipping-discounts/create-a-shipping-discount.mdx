A **shipping discount** lowers the price of shipping rates at checkout when the order matches your conditions. ShipMagic creates it as a regular Shopify discount, so it appears on your Shopify **Discounts** page and on the customer's checkout and order like any other discount.

<Note>
  Shipping discounts change **what customers pay** for rates that already exist. To calculate a new rate, use [Shipping Rates](/shipping-rates/basic-shipping-rate). To hide, rename, or reorder rates, use [Shipping Customizations](/shipping-customizations/basic-shipping-customization).
</Note>

## What you can do

- Give **free shipping**, a **percentage off**, a **fixed amount off**, or **cap shipping** at a set price
- Apply it **automatically** or only when the customer enters a **discount code**
- Limit it with conditions on the cart, products, customer tags, and shipping address — see [Discount Conditions](/shipping-discounts/discount-conditions)
- Target **all rates**, only **rates with a certain name**, or only the **cheapest rate**
- Schedule it with start and end dates, and limit how often a code can be used

## Before you start

A discount reduces the price of rates that already exist, so set those up first. They can be manual Shopify rates, ShipMagic rates, or live carrier rates.

<Tip>
  Like customizations, shipping discounts work on **all Shopify plans** and **don't need product syncing**. Shopify sends product data to the discount at checkout directly.
</Tip>

ShipMagic also needs the **Manage discounts** permission, because it creates the discounts in Shopify. The first time you open **Shipping Discounts**, click **Grant access** on the banner and approve the request.

## Create the discount

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Shipping Discounts and click Create discount">
    From the left navigation panel in the app, open **Shipping Discounts**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a method">
    | Method | Effect |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Automatic discount** | Applies at checkout whenever the conditions match. |
    | **Discount code** | Applies only when the customer enters the code at checkout. Type your own code or click **Generate** for a random one. |

    <Warning>
      Shopify doesn't allow changing the method of an existing discount. To switch, create a new discount.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a title">
    For example `Free shipping over $75`. Unlike a customization's name, this title is **shown to customers** at checkout next to the discount. It also appears in your Shopify admin.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the discount value">
    | Value | Effect on each targeted rate |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Percentage off shipping** | Takes a percentage off, up to 100%. |
    | **Fixed amount off shipping** | Takes a fixed amount off. A rate never goes below 0, so 10 off a 6 rate makes it free. |
    | **Free shipping** | Makes the rate free. |
    | **Set shipping price to** | Caps the rate at the price you enter. Rates above it are reduced to it. Cheaper rates stay as they are. |

    Amounts are in your store currency. When a customer checks out in another currency, the amount is converted automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add conditions (optional)">
    With no conditions, the discount applies to every order. Click **Add condition** to limit it, then choose whether the order must match **All conditions** or **Any condition**.

    You can match on cart subtotal, quantity, and weight; product tag, collection, vendor, title, and price; customer tag; and country or province. See [Discount Conditions](/shipping-discounts/discount-conditions) for every attribute and operator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose which rates it applies to">
    Under **Applies to**:

    | Option | Discounts |
    | --- | --- |
    | **All shipping rates** | Every rate shown at checkout. |
    | **Only rates whose name contains** | Rates whose name contains any of the names you list. Click **Add rate name** for more than one. |
    | **Only the cheapest shipping rate** | The single cheapest rate. |

    <Tip>
      **Partial names match, and matching is case-sensitive.** `Express` matches "Express Shipping" and "UPS Express", but `express` matches neither. Copy the name from checkout rather than typing it from memory.
    </Tip>

    Rates that are already free, such as local pickup, are never discounted. They're also skipped when finding the cheapest rate, so **Only the cheapest shipping rate** always picks the cheapest rate the customer would pay for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set status, limits, combinations, and dates">
    The cards on the right of the form:

    - **Status** — _Enabled_ or _Disabled_. Leave it _Enabled_ for the discount to apply.
    - **Usage limits** (discount codes only) — limit the total number of times the code can be used, and/or limit it to one use per customer.
    - **Combinations** — whether this discount can be combined with **Product discounts** and **Order discounts**.
    - **Active dates** — when the discount starts, and optionally when it ends. Dates are in your store's timezone, shown under the fields.

    See [Managing Shipping Discounts](/shipping-discounts/managing-shipping-discounts) for how each of these behaves.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. ShipMagic creates the discount in Shopify, and it takes effect at checkout straight away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Worked examples

**Free standard shipping over 75**

```text
Method: Automatic discount
Title: Free shipping over $75
Value: Free shipping
Condition 1: Cart Subtotal <> Greater than or equal <> 75
Applies to: Only rates whose name contains: Standard
```

**Half-price express shipping for VIP customers**

```text
Method: Automatic discount
Title: VIP express shipping
Value: Percentage off shipping <> 50
Condition 1: Customer Tag <> Equals <> vip
Applies to: Only rates whose name contains: Express
```

**A code that caps shipping at 5 on furniture**

```text
Method: Discount code <> SHIP5
Title: $5 shipping on furniture
Value: Set shipping price to <> 5
Condition 1: Product Collection <> Equals <> 123456789
             At least one product meets the condition.
Applies to: All shipping rates
Usage limits: Limit to one use per customer
```

## Test your discount

Shipping discounts have their own test mode, separate from rates and customizations.

1. Leave the **Shipping Discounts** toggle off in **Settings → App Modes**
2. Open your storefront and add products that match your conditions
3. At checkout, enter a complete address with `shipmagic` as the **first name**
4. For a discount code, enter the code in the discount field
5. Check that the targeted rates show the discounted price

When it looks right, turn on the **Shipping Discounts** toggle to apply discounts for every customer. Live mode needs the Starter plan or above — see [Going Live](/core-concepts/going-live).
