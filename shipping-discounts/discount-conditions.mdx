description: "Limit shipping discounts by cart, product, customer, and shipping address"

Conditions decide which orders get a shipping discount. A discount with **no conditions** applies to every order.

Once you add a condition, choose whether the order must match **All conditions** or **Any condition**.

<Note>
  Shipping discounts have a single list of attributes. There are no [parameter sets](/ai-tools/different-parameter-sets-for-conditions) like in customizations, so any of the attributes below can be combined in one discount.
</Note>

## Available attributes

| Group | Attribute | Operators |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cart | **Cart Subtotal** | Numeric comparisons |
| Cart | **Cart Quantity** | Numeric comparisons |
| Cart | **Cart Weight** | Numeric comparisons |
| Product | **Product Tag** | Equals, Not equals |
| Product | **Product Collection** | Equals, Not equals |
| Product | **Product Vendor** | Equals, Not equals, Contains, Does not contains, Includes in list, Not includes in list |
| Product | **Product Title** | Equals, Not equals, Contains, Does not contains, Includes in list, Not includes in list |
| Product | **Product Price** | Numeric comparisons, Is empty, Is not empty |
| Customer | **Customer Tag** | Equals, Not equals |
| Shipping address | **Country Code** | Equals, Not equals, Contains, Does not contains, Includes in list, Not includes in list |
| Shipping address | **Province / State Code** | Equals, Not equals, Contains, Does not contains, Includes in list, Not includes in list |
| Other | **Always** | True |

Numeric comparisons are Equals, Not equals, Greater than, Less than, Greater than or equal, and Less than or equal.

<Warning>
  **Product Tag**, **Customer Tag**, and **Product Collection** can each be used in **only one condition** per discount. To match several tags or collections, list them in that one condition, separated by commas.
</Warning>

## Cart conditions

- **Cart Subtotal** — the order value, in your store currency. When a customer checks out in another currency, it's converted before the comparison, so `75` always means 75 in your store currency.
- **Cart Quantity** — the total number of items in the cart.
- **Cart Weight** — the total weight, in your store's weight unit.

<Note>
  Product weights aren't converted between units. If some products have their weight entered in a different unit from your store's, Cart Weight will be off for carts that contain them.
</Note>

## Product conditions

- **Product Tag** — enter one or more tags, separated by commas. _Equals_ matches a product that has **any** of the tags. _Not equals_ matches a product that has **none** of them.
- **Product Collection** — enter collection **IDs**, not names, separated by commas. See [How to Find the ID of a Product Collection](/finding-collection-and-rule-ids).
- **Product Vendor** and **Product Title** — text matches are case-sensitive. For _Includes in list_, separate values with semicolons: `Acme;Globex`.
- **Product Price** — the price of one unit of the product, in your store currency.

### How the cart is evaluated

For each product condition you also choose how much of the cart must satisfy it:

| Option | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **At least one product meets the condition** | Any single product matching is enough. |
| **All products meet the condition** | Every product in the cart must match. |
| **Cart has exactly specified quantity** | Matching products total exactly N units. |
| **Cart has more than specified quantity** | Matching products total more than N units. |
| **Cart has not more than specified quantity** | Matching products total N units or fewer. |
| **Cart has more than specified total price** | Matching products are worth N or more in total (price × quantity). |
| **Cart has not more than total price** | Matching products are worth less than N in total. |

<Tip>
  The total-price options let you base a discount on how much the customer spends **on certain products**, not on the whole cart. For example: free shipping when the customer spends 100 or more on items tagged `bundle`.
</Tip>

## Customer conditions

**Customer Tag** — enter one or more tags, separated by commas. _Equals_ matches a customer who has **any** of the tags.

<Warning>
  Customer tags are only known when the customer is **logged in**. A guest has no tags, so _Equals_ never matches a guest and _Not equals_ always does.
</Warning>

## Shipping address conditions

- **Country Code** — two-letter codes such as `US`, `GB`, or `AU`.
- **Province / State Code** — Shopify's province codes, such as `CA` or `ON`.

For _Includes in list_, separate codes with semicolons: `CA;NV;OR`. See [Country and Province Codes](/country-and-province-codes) for the full list.

## Always

**Always** matches every order. You rarely need it, because a discount with no conditions already applies to every order.

## Examples

**Free shipping on West Coast orders over 50**

```text
Rule must match: All conditions
Condition 1: Country Code <> Equals <> US
Condition 2: Province / State Code <> Includes in list <> CA;OR;WA
Condition 3: Cart Subtotal <> Greater than or equal <> 50
```

**10 off shipping when the cart has 3 or more items from a collection**

```text
Condition 1: Product Collection <> Equals <> 123456789
             Cart has more than specified quantity: 2
```

**Free shipping for wholesale or VIP customers, unless the order is very heavy**

```text
Rule must match: All conditions
Condition 1: Customer Tag <> Equals <> wholesale, vip
Condition 2: Cart Weight <> Less than <> 30
```
