---
title: "Shipping Discount Not Applying"
description: "Work through the causes of a shipping discount not showing at checkout"
icon: "circle-exclamation"
---

Shipping discounts don't need CCS or product syncing, which rules out two common classes of problem. What's left is usually status, mode, conditions, or rate names.

## 1. Is the discount active?

**Check:** the status badge on the **Shipping Discounts** page.

| Status | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| _Disabled_ | Open the discount, set **Status** to _Enabled_, and save. |
| _Scheduled_ | The start date is in the future. Check **Active dates** — they're in your store's timezone. |
| _Expired_ | The end date has passed. Move it, or untick **Set end date**. |
| _Missing in Shopify_ | Open the discount and click **Save** to create it again. |
| _Unknown_ | Click **Grant access** on the banner to give ShipMagic the **Manage discounts** permission. |

## 2. Are you in test mode without the test name?

In test mode, shipping discounts only apply when the checkout's **first name is `shipmagic`**.

**Check:** **Settings → App Modes** — the Shipping Discounts toggle.

**Fix:** use `shipmagic` as the first name when testing, or [go live](/core-concepts/going-live). The toggles are independent — rates and customizations can be live while discounts are still in test mode.

## 3. Do the conditions match?

**Check each one:**

- Is **All conditions** set where you meant **Any condition**?
- For **Customer Tag** — is the customer logged in? A guest has no tags.
- For **Product Collection** — did you enter the collection **ID** rather than its name?
- For product conditions — is the cart-evaluation mode right? _All products meet the condition_ and _At least one product meets the condition_ behave very differently.
- For **Product Vendor** and **Product Title** — text matching is case-sensitive.
- For amounts — they're in your **store currency**, even when the customer pays in another currency.

See [Discount Conditions](/shipping-discounts/discount-conditions).

## 4. Does the rate name match?

With **Only rates whose name contains**, the name must appear in the rate's name exactly, **including capitalization**. `express` doesn't match "Express Shipping".

**Check:** compare the name in your discount against the name shown at checkout, character for character.

**Fix:** copy the name from checkout. If a [shipping customization](/shipping-customizations/basic-shipping-customization) renames the rate, use the **new** name — discounts run after customizations.

## 5. Is the rate already free?

Rates that cost nothing, such as local pickup or an existing free shipping rate, are never discounted. With **Only the cheapest shipping rate**, the discount goes to the cheapest rate the customer would pay for.

## 6. Is another shipping discount winning?

Shopify applies **one shipping discount per order**. If several qualify, the customer gets the biggest saving, and the others don't show.

**Check:** **Shopify Admin → Discounts** for other active shipping discounts — from ShipMagic, from Shopify itself, or from other apps — that also match your test cart.

## 7. Does it combine with the other discounts on the order?

If the order also has a product or order discount, both discounts must allow combining with each other.

**Check:** the **Combinations** card on your shipping discount, and the combination settings of the other discount in Shopify.

## 8. Has the code reached its usage limit?

For discount codes, check the **Usage limits** card. A code limited to one use per customer won't apply to a customer who has already used it.
