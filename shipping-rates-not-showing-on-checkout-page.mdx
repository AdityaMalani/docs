---
title: "Shipping Rates Not Showing On Checkout Page"
description: "Work through the causes of ShipMagic rates failing to appear at checkout"
icon: "circle-exclamation"
---

Work down this list in order — the causes are roughly ordered by how often they turn out to be the problem.

## 1. Is CCS enabled?

Carrier Calculated Shipping is mandatory for any third-party app to return rates. Without it, ShipMagic is never even asked.

**Check:** open the **Shipping Rates** page in the app. If CCS is not enabled you'll see an error message there. No error means CCS is active.

**Fix:** [contact Shopify support](https://help.shopify.com/en#/contact) to have CCS enabled. See the [Installation Guide](/installation-guide#2-ensure-ccs-is-enabled-on-your-store).

## 2. Are you in test mode without the test name?

If the app is in test mode, it only returns rates for checkouts where the **first name is `shipmagic`**. Everyone else sees your normal Shopify rates.

**Check:** **Settings → App Modes**. If the Shipping Rates toggle is off, you're in test mode.

**Fix:** either enter `shipmagic` as the first name when testing, or [go live](/core-concepts/going-live). See [Test mode vs live mode](/test-mode-vs-live-mode).

## 3. Is ShipMagic a rate provider on that profile?

ShipMagic is added to the **General** profile automatically. Custom shipping profiles need it added manually.

**Check:** **Shopify Admin → Settings → Shipping and Delivery**. Open the profile covering the products in your test cart and confirm ShipMagic appears as a rate provider in the relevant zone.

**Fix:** follow [adding ShipMagic to custom profiles](/installation-guide#add-shipmagic-as-a-rate-provider-custom-profiles--zones).

<Warning>
  Products can sit in a different shipping profile than you expect. If some carts get rates and others don't, compare which profile each product belongs to — that's usually the difference.
</Warning>

## 4. Have zones been synced, and does the zone have rates?

**Check:** open your rule. If you see a warning instead of your zones, they haven't been synced. If a zone shows _"No rates. Customers in this zone won't see shipping rates from ShipMagic app"_, that zone has nothing to return.

**Fix:** sync from **Settings → Shipping Zones**, then attach a rate to the zone. See [Adding / Modifying Shipping Zones](/essentials/adding-modifying-shipping-zones).

## 5. Do the conditions actually match your test cart?

A rule that doesn't match produces nothing.

**Check each condition against your cart:**

- Is the rule **Active**?
- For **Product Tag, Type, Title, Barcode, or Compare-at Price** conditions — have you [synced products](/core-concepts/syncing-products) since last editing them?
- For **customer** conditions — are you logged in? Guests have no tags and zero spend.
- Is **All conditions** set where you meant **Any condition**?
- For **ranged rates**, does your cart fall inside a band? A cart above your largest band with no open-ended final band gets nothing.

<Tip>
  Isolate the problem by temporarily creating a rule with a single always-true-ish condition and a fixed rate. If that appears, the issue is your conditions. If it doesn't, the issue is higher up this list.
</Tip>

## 6. Are you seeing a cached rate?

Shopify caches shipping rates per address. Re-testing with the same address shows the old result no matter what you changed.

**Fix:** use a fresh, random address for every test. See [Made changes but still seeing old rates](/made-changes-in-the-rule-but-still-seeing-the-old-rates).

## 7. Did a live carrier fail to respond?

If a rate uses a live carrier and the carrier doesn't respond, that rate produces nothing unless a **Default rate** is set.

**Check:** credentials are current, and a default rate is set on the carrier rate.

**Fix:** set a **Default rate** on every live carrier rate. See the carrier's page — [USPS](/usps), [FedEx](/fed-ex), [Australia Post](/australia-post), [ShipStation](/ship-station), [Shiprocket](/shiprocket), [Stallion Express](/stallion-express), [Direct Freight Express](/direct-freight-express).

## 8. Is an exclusive rule suppressing everything?

An [exclusive](/exclusive-rules) rule that matches skips all lower-priority rules. An exclusive rule with broad conditions at a low priority number can silence your whole rule set.

**Check:** look for exclusive rules with a low priority number and permissive conditions.

## 9. Is a customization hiding the rate?

A shipping rate can be generated correctly and then hidden by a customization.

**Check:** review your customizations for a **Hide** or **Only show** rule matching your test conditions. See [Shipping Customizations Not Working](/shipping-customizations-not-working).
