Stallion Express is a Canadian shipping service, widely used for cross-border shipping into the US. The integration supports package types, signature, and insurance options.

## Getting credentials

Generate an **API Key** from your Stallion Express dashboard under API settings.

## Setting up the rate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add a rate to your zone">
    In your rule, click **Add rate** on the zone you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the calculation parameter to Stallion Express">
    Choose it under the live carrier options.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your API Key">
    Paste the key from your Stallion dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a Package type">
    Select the packaging format — including **Pallet** for freight-scale consignments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set parcel distribution">
    Configure how cart items are distributed into parcels. See [the packing algorithm](/shipping-rates/types-of-rates/carrier-rates/3-d-boxing-algorithm).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose signature and insurance">
    Tick **Signature required** and **Insured** as needed. Both affect the quoted price.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a discount and default rate">
    **Discount (Optional)** reduces every returned rate by a percentage. **Default rate (Optional)** is used if Stallion doesn't respond.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Signature and insurance

These two options change the price Stallion quotes, so set them to match how you actually ship.

- **Signature required** — proof of delivery. Adds cost, reduces disputes.
- **Insured** — declared-value coverage. Adds cost, protects high-value goods.

<Tip>
  If only some of your catalogue warrants signature or insurance, don't enable them globally. Create a second rule with a [product condition](/cart) — for example on price or a `high-value` tag — and enable the options only on that rule's rate.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Enabling these on every rate means every customer pays for coverage most orders don't need, which shows up as an uncompetitive shipping price at checkout.
</Warning>

## Cross-border shipping

Stallion is commonly used to ship from Canada into the United States. Which services return depends on the destination and your account configuration.

If you ship both domestically within Canada and across the border, consider separate rules with [country conditions](/customer-location-and-address) so each is configured appropriately.

## Always set a Default rate

<Warning>
  If Stallion is unavailable and no default rate is set, the customer sees no shipping option and cannot complete checkout.
</Warning>

## Testing

1. Add products with accurate weights and dimensions
2. Check out to a real address, using a fresh address each time
3. Test both a Canadian and a US destination if you ship to both
4. Confirm the price reflects your signature and insurance settings
